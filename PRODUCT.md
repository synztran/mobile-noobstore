# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vietnamese mechanical keyboard enthusiasts — hobbyists and collectors buying, selling, and trading premium mechanical keyboards, switches, keycaps, and accessories. They are mobile-first users (PWA, standalone display) who value community (gacha, rewards, forums) alongside commerce. Context: browsing on mobile during leisure time, researching products, comparing specs, participating in community events.

## Product Purpose

NoobStore is a mobile-first e-commerce storefront for premium mechanical keyboards in Vietnam. It combines retail (product catalog, cart, checkout, payment via QR transfer), services (cleaning, repair booking), community (posts, gacha/lottery, rewards/loyalty), and content (blogs, news). Success = repeat purchases driven by community engagement and a seamless mobile shopping experience.

## Brand Personality

Playful, premium, community. The terracotta/olive palette signals warmth and craft; the gacha/rewards system adds gamified delight; the Manrope typeface and MD3-inspired token system keep the surface polished and professional. Voice is Vietnamese-first, casual but confident.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS cream/beige surfaces (the "warm neutral" AI default)
- Corporate e-commerce templates (Amazon, Shopee clone patterns)
- Overly serious/luxury aesthetics that alienate the hobbyist audience
- Gradient text on every heading, glassmorphism as default, numbered section eyebrows

## Design Principles

1. **Mobile-native first** — Every interaction designed for thumb reach, touch targets, and iOS safe areas. Desktop is secondary.
2. **Community drives commerce** — Gacha, rewards, and community features are first-class, not afterthoughts. They create the stickiness that drives repeat purchases.
3. **Warm precision** — The terracotta palette and MD3 token system create a warm but precise feel. Not cold luxury, not generic warmth.
4. **Progressive disclosure** — Show what's needed, when it's needed. Skeletons for loading, drawers for details, sheets for actions.
5. **Vietnamese-first** — All copy, ARIA labels, and content in Vietnamese. Currency in VND. Addresses in Vietnamese administrative divisions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA target
- `prefers-reduced-motion` already respected globally
- Mobile-first with iOS safe-area handling
- Screen reader support via ARIA labels (Vietnamese)
- No dark mode yet (opportunity for improvement)
